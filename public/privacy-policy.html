READwy Privacy Policy

READwy – Enchanted Academy of Reading · Last updated: 27 November 2026

Who we are

READwy (Enchanted Academy of Reading) is a reading and writing program for learners and their teachers at Emilia Ambalada Poblete Integrated High School. The READwy Android app opens the READwy website (readwy-eapihs.onrender.com) inside the app. This policy explains what information READwy collects, how it is used, and the choices learners, parents, guardians and teachers have.

Privacy contact: cherrylyn.norcio@deped.gov.ph

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address and password, or sign-in with a Google account or an emailed sign-in link. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication; READwy never sees them.
  • Learner profile: Learner Reference Number (LRN), grade, section, the teacher and class the learner belongs to, and the class code used to join.
  • Teacher profile: name, DepEd email address, and the grades and sections the teacher handles.
  • Learning records: assessment results and scores (for example Keypass, Voice Station, Spellscript and the Post-Test), progress in the reading programs, and written answers and essays the learner submits.
  • Teacher notes: intervention reports that teachers write about their learners.
  • LRN check: when a learner signs up, the LRN is checked against the school's class list for that grade and section, and recorded so that one LRN is used by only one account.

Information kept only on the device

  • Voice recordings: read-aloud activities use the microphone only when the learner presses Record. Recordings stay on the device (the learner may save them); they are not uploaded to READwy.
  • Progress and settings are also saved in the browser storage on the device so learners can continue where they left off.

READwy does not show advertising, does not sell personal information, and does not use analytics or tracking tools.

How we use the information

  • To create and secure accounts and let learners and teachers sign in.
  • To place each learner in the right reading program and show their progress.
  • To let a learner's own teacher see that learner's results and write intervention reports.

Only the learner, their assigned teacher and authorized school personnel can see a learner's records.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore; data stored in Singapore) keeps accounts and learning records.
  • Render and Firebase Hosting serve the READwy website.
  • Read-aloud and voice features may use the text-to-speech and speech services built into the device's browser or operating system.

Information is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Children's privacy

READwy is used by learners under 18 as part of their schooling at Emilia Ambalada Poblete Integrated High School, under the supervision of their teachers. Personal information is processed in line with the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Parents and guardians may contact us at the address above with any questions.

How long we keep information

Learner accounts and learning records are kept until the end of the school year, and are then deleted.

Your rights

Learners, parents and guardians may ask to see, correct or delete their personal information by emailing cherrylyn.norcio@deped.gov.ph.

Deleting an account

To delete a READwy account and its data, send an email to cherrylyn.norcio@deped.gov.ph from the email address used for the account (or with the learner's name, grade and section), with the subject "Delete my READwy account". The account and its learning records will be deleted, and you will receive a confirmation by email.

Changes

We will post any changes to this policy on this page and update the date above.
